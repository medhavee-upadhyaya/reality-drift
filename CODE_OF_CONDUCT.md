# Code of Conduct

Reality Drift is committed to a respectful, constructive, and harassment-free community.

Be curious, critique ideas rather than people, cite evidence, and assume good faith. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when behavior harms the community. To report conduct concerns privately, contact the maintainer through the email listed on their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/medhavee-upadhyaya). Reports will be reviewed with discretion.
